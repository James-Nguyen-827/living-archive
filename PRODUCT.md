# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Hiring managers, collaborators, and curious peers who want to understand James's professional work and broader creative practice without being pushed through a conventional job-seeking portfolio.

## Product Purpose

Present James Nguyen's multidisciplinary body of work as an explorable personal portfolio. Visitors freely orbit a responsive habitat, choose an island, and watch a geometric traveler navigate there automatically. The same employment history, writing, projects, interests, and personal context remain available through an immediate conventional index.

## Brand Personality

Curious, architectural, and exacting. The experience should evoke playful discovery, graphic confidence, and a deliberately constructed personal world.

## Anti-references

Generic resume sites, template card grids, photoreal architectural renders, game clones, forced hiring pitches, cream editorial portfolios, glass dashboards, and AI-generated geometry artifacts.

## Design Principles

1. Make exploration rewarding without hiding information.
2. Treat personal interests as part of the creative practice, not filler.
3. Make every spatial route and environmental reaction deterministic and understandable.
4. Offer a fast conventional path beside every experimental interaction.
5. Let the work speak before asking visitors to make contact.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All content and navigation must remain available without WebGL, pointer input, animation, manual locomotion, or color perception. Honor reduced motion and data-saving preferences. The canvas never owns essential content.
