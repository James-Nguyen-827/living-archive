## A temporary rhythm

This prototype treated the schedule less like a calendar and more like a score. Events expand, overlap, and recede as the evening progresses, while a stable text index keeps every item reachable.

The visual language was intentionally temporary: signal colors, oversized timings, and abrupt crops that could change each season without losing the underlying information system.
