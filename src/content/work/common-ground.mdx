## Shared space, distinct voices

Common Ground needed to feel collective without becoming anonymous. A modular wordmark and blunt directional system create continuity, while resident-made imagery changes from place to place.

The web guide behaves like a field sheet: fast, printable, and usable in bright sunlight. High contrast, direct language, and offline-friendly pages matter more than ornamental effects.
