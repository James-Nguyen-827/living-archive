---
title: Notes need weather
summary: A small argument for letting personal archives show age, uncertainty, and change.
publishDate: 2026-03-10
tags: [writing, archives]
---

A portfolio often freezes everything at the moment it looked most impressive. An archive can do something more honest: show which ideas survived, which changed shape, and which have been set aside.

Status belongs close to the work. “Maintained,” “in progress,” and “archived” say more than a stream of silent timestamps.
