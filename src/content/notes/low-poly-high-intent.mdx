---
title: Low poly, high intent
summary: Why fewer geometric decisions demand more care, not less.
publishDate: 2026-05-22
tags: [3D, craft]
---

Low-poly work exposes proportion. Without texture noise or tiny detail, a bad join has nowhere to hide. Platforms need plausible weight, stairs need a repeatable rise, and every silhouette needs to read from more than one angle.

Constraint is useful here. A square grid, a small family of modules, and four authored views turn visual QA into something we can validate rather than merely eyeball.
