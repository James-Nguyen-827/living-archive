---
title: A map can be a menu
summary: Notes on spatial navigation that remains useful when the novelty wears off.
publishDate: 2026-07-04
tags: [navigation, interaction]
---

Spatial interfaces are memorable, but memory alone does not make them usable. A good map reveals relationships. A good menu reduces the cost of reaching a known destination. The useful design is often both at once.

The rule for this archive is simple: the world can reward wandering, but the index should never make someone wander when they already know where they are going.
