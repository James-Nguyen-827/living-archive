## Practical AI literacy on campus

As a ChatGPT Campus Ambassador, I promoted awareness and responsible use of AI tools through workshops, demonstrations, and outreach. The role focused on helping students and faculty understand where these tools could be useful in academic work.

## Listening alongside presenting

I gathered feedback from students and faculty about their needs, questions, and practical use cases. Those conversations informed a grounded approach to discussing AI adoption rather than treating the technology as a one-size-fits-all solution.
